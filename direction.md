# VALLE DEL SOL — direction

> The canon. The site grows from this file.
> Founding brief: 2026-09-06. Not-yet-canon material lives in `seeds.md`.

## The law

> **The mountain is the marketing.**

The land is the institution. The story of building it funds it. The site is that story, not a brochure.

Chingón is the still doorway. This is the journey.

## Two URLs

- `/` — still splash. The dusk lockup. Almost no chrome.Sendable now.
- `/vision` — camera through one continuous land. Scroll is the verb; type is the caption. Does not replace `/` until it earns it.

No nav. A faint mark from `/` to `/vision` is enough. Sound later.

## The world

One place. The mountain never cuts to a new scene. Light, camera, and fog tell the story.

The parcel is **hard**, behind a mountain, steep, urban-edge — not a gentle ranch. Camera follows that: approach a ridge, crest, descend a switchback, hold. Built things, if they appear, sit on a lower apron as abstract geometry (posts, a ramada line). Upper slopes stay wild in the frame.

The listing aerial is a path hint, not a texture.

## Feeling

Quiet, mysterious, tactile, grounded, elegant, sparse, cinematic. Ecological and computational in the same breath. Slightly mythic. Masculine without machismo. Spiritual without wellness branding. Futuristic without sci-fi cliché. Natural without cottagecore. Mexican / Californian without decorative cultural cliché.

Grown, not decorated.

## Stack

Vite + React. Three.js + React Three Fiber. GSAP ScrollTrigger as the timeline. One `progress` scalar, 0 → 1.

WebGL is the substrate. Do not add Lenis, Theatre, Cannon, photoreal GLBs, or a bloom stack until a specific beat demands it.
